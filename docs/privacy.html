SPhoto 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 20 日

一句话：SPhoto 不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何数据。它没有联网能力，所有照片处理都只发生在你的设备本地。

我们收集哪些信息

没有。SPhoto 不收集任何个人信息，不做用户识别，不做行为统计，也不含任何第三方分析或广告 SDK。

照片如何被使用

  • App 需要相册读写权限，用于展示你的照片缩略图和大图，以及在你确认后删除照片。
  • 照片只在你的设备上读取和显示，不会被复制、上传或传输到任何服务器。
  • 「回收站」是应用内的暂存区。它只记录照片在系统相册里的本地标识符（不含照片内容），保存在设备本地的 App 沙盒中。
  • 当你点击「删除 N 张」并在系统弹窗中确认后，这些照片会被移入 iOS 系统相册的「最近删除」，由系统按其规则保留一段时间。此后 SPhoto 不再持有它们的任何信息。

网络与数据传输

SPhoto 不发起任何网络请求。它不包含网络通信代码，因此不存在数据外传的可能。

数据存储与删除

App 在本地存储的唯一数据是回收站里待删照片的标识符列表。卸载 SPhoto 即可彻底清除这份数据。卸载不会影响你系统相册里的照片。

追踪

SPhoto 不进行 App 跟踪透明度（ATT）意义上的任何追踪，也不与任何第三方共享数据。

儿童隐私

SPhoto 不收集任何信息，因此也不会收集儿童的信息。

政策变更

若本政策有更新，我们会修改本页并更新顶部的日期。

联系方式

如对本政策有疑问，请通过 App Store 页面提供的支持渠道联系我们。

SPhoto Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

In one line: SPhoto collects nothing, uploads nothing, and shares nothing. It has no networking capability; all photo handling happens locally on your device.

Information We Collect

None. SPhoto collects no personal information, performs no user identification or behavioural analytics, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

How Your Photos Are Used

  • The app requests photo library read/write access to display thumbnails and full-size images, and to delete photos after you confirm.
  • Photos are read and displayed only on your device. They are never copied, uploaded, or transmitted to any server.
  • The in-app "Recycle Bin" is a staging area. It stores only the local identifiers of photos (never image content), kept in the app's local sandbox on your device.
  • When you tap "Delete N" and confirm in the system dialog, those photos are moved to the iOS Photos app's "Recently Deleted" album and retained by the system under its own policy. SPhoto retains no information about them afterwards.

Network and Data Transmission

SPhoto makes no network requests. It contains no networking code, so there is no path by which data could leave your device.

Data Storage and Deletion

The only data stored locally is the list of identifiers for photos staged in the Recycle Bin. Uninstalling SPhoto removes it completely. Uninstalling does not affect the photos in your system photo library.

Tracking

SPhoto performs no tracking as defined by App Tracking Transparency, and shares no data with any third party.

Children's Privacy

SPhoto collects no information of any kind, and therefore collects no information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date shown above.
